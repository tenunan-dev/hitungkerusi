# GE16 Battleground Seats Analysis — Malaysia

**Compiled:** 3 August 2026 | **Scope:** 36 federal seats with GE15 winning margin < 5% of valid votes
**Data:** GE15 results (EC via Wikipedia), voter demographics (ElectionData.MY GE15 roll, CC0), current composition (Jun 2026), by-elections (MECo).

## 1. Executive Summary

GE16 will be decided in **36 battleground seats** — the federal constituencies where the GE15 winning margin was under 5%. These seats are the swing set: the coalition that converts the most of them wins government. The current government (151 seats) has a 41-seat working majority over the 69-seat opposition bloc, but its hold depends heavily on this battleground set.

**Key structural facts:**
- **8 super-marginal seats** (<1% margin) — any swing flips them
- **13 high-risk seats** (1–2.5%) and **15 watch seats** (2.5–5%)
- **PN holds 11** battlegrounds, **PH 9**, **BN 7**, independents 4, GPS 2, GRS/MUDA/KDM 1 each
- **6 battleground seats have already changed hands** since GE15 (defections): Gua Musang, Tuaran, Labuan, Muar, Tenom, Tanjong Karang
- **Perak (6) and Pahang (6)** are the most contested states, followed by Selangor (5) and Johor (5)

## 7. Strategic Implications for GE16

### Coalition math
- Current government holds 151/222. If **PN** were to win **all 36 battlegrounds** it would not reach government alone (needs ~112 for majority; the battleground set includes BN/GPS/GRS-held seats that would not necessarily fall to PN).
- The realistic fight is over the **11 PN-held + 4 independent-held** battlegrounds: if PH/BN/govt allies flip 9 of those 15, the government cushions its majority against the 2 vacant seats and internal defections.

### Demographic signals
- **Youth (18–30) = 30% of the national electorate.** In battleground seats, youth share averages ~9–12%. The Undi18 block is the single largest swing variable; parties that mobilise young voters convert super-marginals.
- **Malay-majority battlegrounds** (mostly PN-held, 80–96% Malay) are the green-wave frontier; **mixed urban battlegrounds** (Selangor/Johor, 30–60% Chinese) are PH's defence line.

### East Malaysia kingmakers
- Sabah battlegrounds (Putatan, Tuaran, Tenom, Kimanis-adjacent) sit inside GRS/UPKO/KDM/WARISAN turf — post-GE15 realignments (UPKO→GRS) make these the most unpredictable.
- Sarawak GPS holds 2 watch seats (Lubok Antu, Mas Gading) — GPS's 23 seats remain the coalition arithmetic's bedrock.

### By-election validation
- Recent federal by-elections (Kemaman Dec 2023, Pulai Sep 2023, K. Terengganu Aug 2023) show PAS holding its east-coast fortress and PH retaining in mixed seats — consistent with the battleground map above.
