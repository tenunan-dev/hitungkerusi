# Malaysia Election Study Ecosystem — Think Tanks, Research Institutes & Organisations

**Project:** Malaysia General Election | **Compiled:** 3 August 2026 | **Sources:** Web research (organisation websites, news, academic publications)

A directory of organisations that conduct or publish election studies, polling, electoral reform analysis, and related research on Malaysian elections. Organised by type.

---

## 1. Dedicated Opinion Polling Houses (political surveys / election projections)

| Organisation | Founded | Focus | Website / Contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Merdeka Center for Opinion Research** | 1999 | Malaysia's most cited independent pollster; voter sentiment, approval ratings, GE projections (predicted PH 82 seats at GE15) | merdeka.org |
| **Ilham Centre** | ~2014 | Election projections & voter behaviour studies (predicted PH 86 at GE15); first-time voter analysis; state polls | facebook.com/ILHAMResearchCentre; research chief: Assoc. Prof Dr Mohd Yusri Ibrahim |
| **EMIR Research** | 2019 | Data-driven think tank; GE15 seat projections (multi-technique), policy research — Engagement, Moderation, Innovation, Rigour | emirresearch.com |
| **Invoke Malaysia** | 2016 | Data/analytics firm founded by Rafizi Ramli; electoral-roll-based voter modelling, phone polls, seat projections | en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invoke_Malaysia |
| **O2 Research** | — | Polling & survey research; GE15 vote-share surveys | o2research.my (via media reports) |
| **UCSI Poll Research Centre** | 2015 | Academic-affiliated public opinion & survey research; political sentiment polls | ucsipoll.org.my |
| **Kajidata Research** | ~2018 | Polling & data analytics (pre-GE15 era) | via Malay Mail / media reports |
| **Vodus** | — | Data-science polling; publishes GE15 poll post-mortems comparing predictions | vodus.com |
