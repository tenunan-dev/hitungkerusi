# Parliamentary Seats in Malaysia — Data Reference

**Project:** Malaysia General Election
**Compiled:** 3 August 2026
**Scope:** Dewan Rakyat (House of Representatives) federal parliamentary constituencies

---

## 1. Overview

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chamber | Dewan Rakyat (House of Representatives), lower house of Parliament |
| Members | 222 MPs (one per federal constituency) |
| Electoral system | First-past-the-post (FPTP), single-member constituencies |
| Constitutional basis | Article 44 (chamber), Article 46 (seat allocation per state) |
| Term | Up to 5 years; auto-dissolution 19 December 2027 (15th Parliament) |
| Voting age | 18 (lowered from 21 via Constitution (Amendment) Act 2019) |
| MP salary | RM25,700/month (including allowances) |
| Constituency codes | Federal = P.xxx (e.g., P.102 Bangi); State = N.xx |
| Total districts | 222 federal + 600 state constituencies |
| Last redelineation | 2018 (boundaries used in GE14/GE15) |
| Redelineation cycle | Not less than 8 years (Art. 113 & 13th Schedule) |
| Next election (GE16) | Latest possible polling date 17 February 2028 |

Key rules: federal constituencies may not cross state borders; each federal seat contains 2–6 state constituencies (except Federal Territories, which have no state assembly); rural weightage applies per Schedule 13 Part 2(c).

## 5. Redelineation & Seat Expansion Status (2023–2026)

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Mar 2018 | Last full redelineation (used for GE14 & GE15); 222 seats unchanged in number |
| 2019 | Voting age lowered to 18 (Undi18); ~5.8M new voters added to roll |
| Jul 2019 | Sabah state seats increased 60 → 73 (state level, not federal) |
| Sep 2023 | EC announced review; government approved in principle a redelineation increasing federal seats 222 → 235 |
| Dec 2023 | EC deputy chair clarified no formal redelineation approval yet given |
| 8 Aug 2025 | Sarawak state seats increased 82 → 99 (final; state level) |
| Nov 2025 | PM Anwar confirms plan to add federal seats for Sabah & Sarawak before GE16; Sarawak expected **+12 seats at minimum**; no 1/3 (one-third) commitment "for now" |
| After Mar 2026 | EC may begin peninsula redelineation review (Tebrau & other oversized seats flagged) |
| Feb 2026 | Debate: should new seats be created given 50 small seats vs. oversized urban seats? |

**Current status (as of Aug 2026):** 222 federal seats remain in force. Redelineation expected to be completed before GE16, adding seats especially in Sabah, Sarawak, and possibly Selangor/Johor (e.g., Bangi, Tebrau). Sarawak's federal seat count (currently 31) could rise to ~43; Sabah (25) could rise to ~32 if proposals for East Malaysian representation toward 30–35% proceed.
