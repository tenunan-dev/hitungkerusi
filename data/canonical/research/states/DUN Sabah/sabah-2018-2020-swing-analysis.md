## Interpretation for GE16 modelling

- GRS's 2020 coalition built ~60% of its seats from former-BN-family baselines — the 'green-tsunami' framing in Sabah is really a Borneo-based realignment, not PN-style swing.
- WARISAN's floor is the 14 retained seats plus pockets; its 2020→GE16 trajectory needs the Malay-Muslim Bajau/Suluk belt (east coast) treated separately from west-coast UMNO seats.
- The 6 DAP→WARISAN seats are the most structurally unstable (all with mixed Chinese/Kadazan electorates in the west coast).
- New-seat estimates performed at 4/14 bloc-level — treat those 14 as high-uncertainty in any seat-level model; use bloc-level only.
