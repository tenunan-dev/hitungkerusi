## Notes
- **PN** governs Kedah, Kelantan, Terengganu, Perlis (the "green wave" states) and leads Perak numerically.
- **BN** governs Johor (48/56 — its strongest state) and Malacca.
- **PH** leads Penang (27/40) and Selangor (32/56 with BN support).
- **Negeri Sembilan** (Aug 2026): BN 18 + PN 7 = 25/36 government (MB Ismail bin Lasim, UMNO); PH 11 opposition.
- **Sabah** (Nov 2025): WARISAN won most seats (25) but GRS-led coalition (GRS 22 + BN 6 + PBS 6 + UPKO 3 + STAR 2 = 39) forms government.
- **Sarawak** GPS holds 76/82 — a two-thirds-plus fortress.
- **Pahang** is the only hung-parliament state (BN 17, PN 17, PH 8); BN+PH coalition governs.
